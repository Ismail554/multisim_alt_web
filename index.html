How to use CircuitBench

  • Place a part: click it in the left panel, then click on the canvas. Press R to rotate, Esc to cancel.
  • Wire: drag from any pin or wire end. To start a wire from the middle of another wire, press W first. Crossing wires only connect where a dot is shown.
  • Edit values: select a part and change it in the right panel. Values accept suffixes: 4.7k, 10u, 100n, 2M.
  • Switches and logic inputs: click to toggle.
  • Measure: put a Scope probe on a wire to plot it; use the Voltmeter across parts and the Ammeter in series. Select a part to see its live voltage and current.
  • Ground: analog circuits need a Ground symbol as the 0 V reference.
  • ICs: VCC and GND pins on 74xx chips do not need to be connected. Unconnected control inputs sit at their inactive level.
  • View: scroll to pan, Ctrl+scroll (or pinch) to zoom, right-drag to pan.
  • Shortcuts: Space run/pause, Del delete, Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y undo/redo, Ctrl+C/Ctrl+V copy/paste.
